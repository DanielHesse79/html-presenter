deck-stage

HTML decks,
with a real
presenter view.

Authoring

One <section> per slide.

Write ordinary HTML and CSS. The deck is authored at a fixed size — 1920×1080 here — and scaled to fit the screen it lands on, so what you design is what shows.

Optional

Sound, per slide.

Add data-sound="clip.mp3" to any slide. It plays on arrival and stops when you navigate away. data-sound-loop and data-sound-volume are there if you need them.

Presenting

Press P.

The presenter window opens with your notes, the next note, a slide counter and an elapsed-time clock. Drag it to your second screen — navigation syncs in both directions.
