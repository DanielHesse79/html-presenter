deck-stage

HTML decks,
with a real
operator panel.

Authoring

One <section> per slide.

Write ordinary HTML and CSS. The deck is authored at a fixed size — 1920×1080 here — and scaled to fit the screen it lands on, so what you design is what shows.

Optional

Sound, per slide.

This slide is playing a looping tone. Add data-sound="clip.mp3" to any slide and it starts on arrival, then stops when you leave. Pull the fader in the panel and you will hear this one follow it.

Presenting

The laptop is the console.

Notes, a big pausable clock, a per-slide time plan you can retype on the night, master volume and a blackout key. This window shows only slides. Navigation syncs in both directions.

Appendix

The slide you hoped nobody would ask for.

Marked data-appendix. It never comes up by pressing right, carries no time budget, and does not move the pace. The clock keeps running while you are here, so the panel shows what the detour is costing.

Appendix

Arrows work inside the appendix.

Stepping stays in the band you are in, so a related pair of backups can be paged through without falling back into the talk. Escape, or Back in the panel, returns you.
